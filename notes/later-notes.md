Ideas for structure:

* 3rd person limited perspective
* Detailed but not boring detail
* Don't be afraid to kill off characters, even from their perspective
* Keep it grounded and gritty and real
* Magic is limited and not always the best option
* No crazy universe, everything is here on this world
* Sexual aspects are limited but present
* Wizard is political and egotistical but ambitious and often successful. Vibe of a smug smile you want to punch off his face
* Boy struggles to know what's right, what's true. Gets stuck over thinking and doubting himself
   * Could be cool to start by following a boy from the village, but then he dies at the hand of the actual main character. So you learn about the main character only from this guys perspective for the first while.
   * Part of this could be this phrase that their training group lives by that this boy thinks about. But then when we go to the other character, he has a different perspective
* Also follow a queen? Incredibly smart, very detail oriented. Mostly frustrated with people who don't believe in her, doubt her capabilities no matter what she does. Not a sexism thing?
* Dragon is an old man who is happy with the humble life he has lived and doesn't really yearn for more, especially if he risks losing what he has
* Many kingdoms ruled by ambitious men and women. Wizards are fiercely loyal to their kingdom and very patriotic. No brotherhood among Magic users, only by country. Except one who wants to unite them all more to bang against the coming apocalypse.
* Many doubt the apocalypse, because most dragons are lazy and only guard what they have. Short time of fierce productivity and power and ambition. Just after their adolescence
* Maybe have one king constantly seeking for a way to have his soldiers bond with dragons. Exhausts his kingdoms resources and men capturing dragons and wounding men to make the bond. At the end of storyline, he finally succeeds when his men turn on him, chop off his arms and shoves him to be eaten by a dragon. The king and the dragon bond.
    * Should he then live? Dragon eats his men and then they escape? His kingdom has turned against him. Is it too much to have both of his arms gone?
