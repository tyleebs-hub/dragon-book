Rough Plot:
The book follows a protagonist who communicates telepathically with dragons and teams up with one of them to expand their “domain.” But it didn't start that way. protagonist was enlisted by a wizard from a large human kingdom to help them all prepare for this evil dragon apocalypse. 

Something went wrong in training and the protagonist was exiled or just left. 

The wizard recruited him from a village. He was fighting and training with other boys. He had resistance to training with the wizard but there was some reason he had to go with him. I think it was because of his nascent talent or spark with magic.

The wizard wanted to teach him that his physical prowess and will weren't enough to solve problems so he set up an elaborate ruse to teach him a lesson. Hired bandits were supposed to stop their carriage on their way to the capital, beat up the guards and this boy then the wizard would show off his prowess and "his way" of doing things.

But something went wrong and the wizard was in a deep slumber and/or the bandits came at the wrong time. Boy couldn't wake him up, heard the bandits, bandits were more aggressive than wizard wanted and killed guards, boy fought them off / killed quite a few. Horses and guards and bandits all got killed.

Wizard woke up to find the mess and then they had to keep going on the journey on foot. Could be fun to out hear that the wizard was communing with his comrades in this slumber and boasting about the talent of this new boy that he had personally found.

** can't remember if he separated from the wizard here or went on to the school. Could be a separate idea or book but I do remember one storyline was that he started training but didn't have much luck in his studies. Wasn't doing well. One of the big powerful wizards/professors found a dragon cave not far from the school, closer than usual. They took the whole school to go find and kill the dragon. Show the students the power of the school and power of magic. Boy went along but I think here he started to sympathize with the beast. 

I think here he was actually among the front lines and had special anti-dragon armor with some other students. Serving as fodder basically, drawing out dragon. Didn't see much action as the dragon flew out of the cave and above everyone.

Dragon laid waste to a whole bunch of the school. 

Not sure what happened after that. I think he got knocked out, school retreated. But dragon was injured. He got up and saw the dragon licking his wounds basically. Went to go finish him off, walked underneath the dragon to slip his sword into a wound. But a drop of blood from the dragon fell into a wound of the boy. Bloods mixed and they became bonded.

Now this guy has access to the same power/magic of the dragon.

Dragon tried but failed to kill the boy. Bonding knocked them both out but dragon woke up first. Carried the boy to a new cave further away before more wizards could come. Set the boy in the back of a cave.

** part here where they explore the bond, figure out what's going on.

They conquer other dragons and territories, growing in magic and power in preparation for an apocalyptic event where evil dragons will attack humanity. 

The protagonist builds and fortifies a fortress to prepare for the attack, drawing criticism from his mentor (possibly a wizard), who one day comes to his fortress and confronts him but is countered by the protagonist’s argument that he’s done more to prepare than anyone else.

At one point, the protagonist launches an attack on a human castle, though i don't remember the reasons why or the timing, and his dragon partner is injured or captured during the conflict. This loss causes the protagonist to lose much of his power until he gets back into his own domain.
