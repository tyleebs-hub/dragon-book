## Ch 01: The Drill Ground (Hob)
**Summary:** Dawn drill on Maddoc's ground at Kell's Ford. Maddoc pairs Hob with Bram; Hob goes down twice, gets up both times, and on the third exchange Bram's ash staff splits along its length against a soft blow. Hob's ears ring, his teeth ache, he tastes struck iron. Maddoc says "Ash doesn't split like that," looks hard at Bram, and moves on. At the river Hob confronts Bram, who denies pulling the blow; Hob decides Bram broke the staff on purpose to end the bout (he is wrong). At dusk a rider in Caldreth grey crosses on the ferry: Master Orvane of the king's school is two or three days behind, looking at boys in villages that train. Asked if any are worth looking at, Hob says only himself, and does not mention Bram.

**New facts:**
- Phrase and call-and-response: "What do we do?" "Never stay down." "Why?" "Because down is where they kill you."
- Hob: 15, second son of the ferryman. Family: father (ferryman, unnamed), mother (unnamed, soft toward Tobin and Bram, sat with Bram's dying mother), blind grandmother "Nan," eldest brother Jory (inherits the ferry), sister Nell (5), two more younger siblings. Sleeps on the floor by the fire. Has been to Ferrin market three times, never further. Wants the levy.
- Bram: 16, a hand taller than anyone, works the tannery pits with Tobin since age nine. Washes upstream and apart. Never hits the head or face; Pim says Maddoc took him aside about it three summers ago. Has never lost a bout; Maddoc stopped sparring him two summers ago. Said eleven words to Hob (Hob counted).
- Maddoc: three fingers on the left hand, stiff right leg, served from 18 to 30, home 20 years, paid in flour and firewood. Never speaks of the wars. Noticed the staff and said nothing beyond "Ash doesn't split like that."
- Other boys: Dace (loud, took a lump from Maddoc), Wat and Pim (brothers who argue), Ansel (small, quick), three unnamed. Eight boys total.
- Bram's spark, as seen from outside: staff splits with a sound like river ice; high ringing in the ears; aching teeth; taste of struck iron.
- Caldreth's badge: a black heron on a white ground. The king's school is at Dunmere. Levy comes up the Ferrin road every third or fourth year; last came when Hob was 12 (overdue).
- Places: Ferrin (market town, 9 miles down the road), Marran's alehouse, Jorrey the smith.
- The rider: lean, grey coat, heron badge, unnamed. Told Hob "Maybe. We'll see."

**Setups needing payoff:**
- Hob kept silent about Bram to the rider and does not want to take it back. His jealousy is now live.
- Maddoc knows something about Bram (the look, the no-head-strikes rule). Should surface with Orvane in Ch 2 or in Bram's Ch 4.
- Hob's reading of Bram (decides everything, pulls blows on purpose) is "wrong in every part" per narration; Ch 4 must show the truth from inside.
- Bram looked at his own hand "as if it belonged to somebody else." Pay off at Hob's death.
- Hob's mother's bread to the tannery; Hob never asked inside. Possible beat in Ch 4 when Bram leaves Tobin.
- Bram's boot cord: split leather, tied with cord. Small texture for the road chapters.


## Ch 02: The Heron (Hob)
**Summary:** Two days of village preparation. Hob carries bread to the tannery and, for the first time, goes round to the pits; Bram says he is not going anywhere and that he knows what the wizard wants. Orvane arrives in a black four-horse carriage with Fenn the outrider and two soldiers, and recognizes Maddoc as Third Foot, at the Narrows under Harrow. That night at Marran's he tells the village what dragons are, puts out the fire with a gesture (his hand shakes afterward; only Hob sees), relights it from his finger, describes the spark's signs (things break, dogs dislike him, teeth ache, a taste), and asks if anything odd has happened. Hob keeps silent a second time; Dace blurts "Bram's staff." Orvane learns Hob was the one fighting Bram and asks to see the boys fight tomorrow. In the street after midnight Maddoc tells Hob the wizard will not pick him, that he has known about Bram for three years ("since the pot"), that he told Bram never to strike the head because he has seen once before what an untrained spark does. In the morning Orvane watches only Bram, tells Hob kindly that he will make a sergeant, and asks who fights Bram best. Maddoc names Hob. The chapter ends on "Go."

**New facts:**
- Hallam: reeve for eleven years, can read. Marran keeps the alehouse. Old Garan's father once saw a dragon asleep on a rock in the hills past Ferrin. Dace's surname is Miller.
- Fenn: Orvane's outrider, the rider from Ch 1. Gave Hob a dry nod.
- Two soldiers escort the carriage: an older one with grey in his beard who looks at the village like a field to hold, and a young one, barely older than Jory, who keeps touching his sword hilt and grinned at a girl. Unnamed. These are the guards who die in the ambush.
- Orvane: about fifty, middling height, soft, well-kept, good black coat with fine collar stitching, rings, close-trimmed grey beard, thinning grey hair, round pleasant clever face, warm measuring smile. Voice carries without being raised. Claims to have met four dragons, three of them harmless. Says he was at the Narrows "after" and "saw what was left."
- Orvane's magic has a visible cost: after putting out and relighting the fire his right hand trembles and he hides it.
- Orvane's account of dragons (his claim, not confirmed fact): most are harmless for centuries; a season of wanting comes a few years in a life of centuries, near a predictable age; a great many dragons hatched within a few years of one another long ago; the king believes, the king's father did not; Orvane has spent twenty years and much of the king's money on it. It has happened in Caldreth twice in living memory.
- Signs of the spark per Orvane: sound things break near him when his blood is up; animals (dogs especially) dislike him; milk turns; nearby people's teeth ache and there is a taste.
- Maddoc: Third Foot, under Harrow, at the Narrows. Has known about Bram for three years, "since the pot" (unexplained). Told Bram never to strike the head because he has seen once what an untrained spark does. Expects Tobin will let Bram go and the village will be glad.
- Hob has now kept silent about Bram twice. The village and Orvane know Hob is the one who fought Bram when the staff broke.
- Orvane to Hob, in front of the village: "You will make a sergeant, if the levy doesn't kill you first."
- Bram to Hob at the pits: "I'm not going anywhere." "I know what he wants." Tobin seen: grey, leather apron, curved knife, weathered face, silent.

**Setups needing payoff:**
- "Since the pot": whatever Maddoc saw three years ago. Bram's Ch 4 can show it.
- Maddoc has seen an untrained spark before, once. The Narrows? Something from his own past. Do not resolve without the author.
- Orvane and Maddoc share history around the Narrows. Orvane may use it in Ch 4.
- The two soldiers now have faces for the ambush in Ch 5 to 6.
- Orvane's trembling hand: magic costs him. Keep consistent in every Orvane scene.
- Hob's conviction that Bram has "already decided" goes into the bout. Ch 3 and Ch 4 must break it.
- Dace said the words. He will carry that after Hob dies.
