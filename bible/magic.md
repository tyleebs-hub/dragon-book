# Magic

Read this before every chapter that touches magic, the bond, or the domain. This is where drift happens.

## General rules
- Magic is limited, costly, and strange. It is often not the best option.
- Wizard magic requires a natural spark plus long training.
- Wizards can enter a deep slumber to commune with other wizards at a distance. While in it, they are hard or impossible to wake.

## The dragon bond
- Formed when a dragon's blood enters a human's open wound and the bloods mix.
- Bram and Vharis's bond happened by accident: Bram stood under the wounded dragon to stab it, and a drop of its blood fell into his wound.
- Forming the bond knocked both of them unconscious.
- The bonded human gains access to the dragon's power and magic.
- Bonded human and dragon communicate telepathically. **Decided:** Bram speaks only with Vharis. Other dragons he senses loosely, as energy or pressure, with no words. It is enough to know a dragon is near, or caged, or in pain, not what it thinks.
- The bonded human's power depends on the dragon. When Vharis is injured or captured, Bram loses most of his power until he returns to his own domain.

## Forced bonds (the bonding king)
- The king wounds men and captures dragons trying to recreate the bond. It almost always fails.
- His own bond succeeds when he is maimed and thrown to the dragon to be eaten.

**Decided: what makes a bond take.** Both parties must be at the edge of death when the bloods mix.
- Bram: bleeding out on adrenaline after the hunt, not knowing how badly he is hurt. Vharis: gutted by the school's attack.
- The king: dying, both arms (or one) cut off, thrown to a starved and broken caged dragon.
- The king's hundreds of failures: he kept his men alive and his dragons healthy, protecting the two things the bond requires him to risk.
- Nobody in the story knows this rule at the start. It is discovered, not taught.

## The domain
- Bram and Vharis claim territory as their "domain," expanding it by conquering other dragons.
- Within the domain, Bram is at full strength.
- At his peak, Bram can fly using his own magic, but only inside the domain's boundaries.
- Outside the domain, he is much weaker, especially if separated from Vharis.

**Open questions**
- How is a domain claimed? By defeating its previous dragon? By physical marking? By time spent?
- Can Bram feel its edges?
- Does the domain strengthen Vharis too, or only Bram?
- How does Bram fly: grown wings, or wingless levitation? (Notes say "grow wings" as a possibility.)

## Wizard magic as shown (Ch 2)
- Orvane put out a built-up hearth fire with a raised hand and no word, then lit a flame on his fingertip to relight it. Afterward his hand shook. Small, deliberate, costly.
- Orvane's list of how an untrained spark shows: sound things break near the child when his blood is up; dogs dislike him; milk turns; people nearby feel their teeth ache and taste something. He says the child usually does not know.

## Bram's spark (before the bond)
- Nascent and untrained. Shows itself when his blood is up, in small wrong things around him rather than anything he aims.
- Established in prose (Ch 1): a sound ash staff splitting along its grain against a lighter blow; ringing ears and aching teeth in the boy facing him; a taste of struck iron in the air. Maddoc has noticed and said nothing.
- Established in prose (Ch 3): when his blood is fully up the signs come before the blow, not with it: iron taste, aching teeth, ringing, hair standing on the arms, a sense of the ground leaning toward him. The blow itself need not be hard. It killed Hob by rupturing something along his side "the way the ash had gone," and split Hob's staff at the same moment. Bram was looking at his own hand just before, grey-faced; he did not aim it.
