# Characters

All names are working names. Change freely; update everywhere when you do.
Items marked **[suggestion]** came from Claude, not the author's notes. Treat them as open until the author approves.

## POV characters
1. **Tam**, village boy (opening chapters only; dies at Bram's hand)
2. **Bram**, protagonist
3. **The queen** (working name Queen Ysolde)
4. Possibly others later (the bonding king? Orvane?). Ask before adding.

---

## Bram (protagonist)
- Village boy, physically strong, trained by fighting and sparring with other boys.
- Has a nascent spark of magic. That spark is why he must go with the wizard even though he resists.
- **Inner life:** struggles to know what is right and what is true. Overthinks, doubts himself, gets stuck. Outwardly he can look decisive and dangerous (especially through Tam's eyes); inwardly he second-guesses constantly.
- The wizard wants to teach him that physical prowess and will are not enough. The story partly proves the wizard right and partly wrong.
- Kills for the first time (or again; see Tam) defending the carriage from bandits. It marks him.
- Does poorly in his studies at the school.
- Begins to sympathize with the dragon during the school's hunt.
- After the bond: can speak telepathically with dragons and draws on his dragon's power.
- Rides his own horse or walks most of the time, even after he can fly.
- Later: builds and holds a fortress; attracts human followers.

**Open questions**
- Age at recruitment? (Suggest 14 to 16.)
- Family? Who does he leave behind?
- How does he leave the school: exile, choice, or simply vanishing after the bond?

## Tam (opening POV)
- A boy from Bram's village and training group. We meet Bram only through Tam's eyes at first.
- Tam lives by a phrase the training group repeats. When the POV shifts to Bram, Bram reads the same phrase differently.
- Tam dies at Bram's hand.
- **[suggestion]** Tam's death happens in a sparring match or fight that goes too far. That killing could be the "reason he had to go" with the wizard: the village can't keep him, and Orvane offers a way out (or uses it as leverage). It also explains Bram's resistance and his doubt.

**Open questions**
- What is the training group's phrase?
- Accident, or something Bram chose?
- How many chapters does Tam carry before he dies? (Suggest 2 to 3.)

## Master Orvane (the wizard, mentor)
- Serves a large human kingdom. Fiercely loyal to it, like all wizards.
- Political, egotistical, ambitious, and often successful. The vibe: a smug smile you want to punch off his face. He is usually right enough to be unbearable.
- Recruits gifted youths to prepare for the coming dragon apocalypse.
- Stages a fake bandit ambush to humble Bram. It goes wrong.
- During the ambush he is in a deep magical slumber, communing with fellow wizards and boasting about the boy he personally found.
- Later confronts Bram at his fortress and criticizes him. Bram counters that he has done more to prepare than anyone.

**Open questions**
- Is Orvane the one wizard who wants to unite magic users across kingdoms, or is that someone else? (See world.md.)
- Does he ever admit the ambush was staged? When, and to whom?

## Vharis (the dragon)
- An old dragon. In spirit, an old man content with the humble life he has lived. He doesn't yearn for more, especially if reaching for it risks what he has.
- Lairs in a cave unusually close to the school.
- Lays waste to much of the school's expedition, but is wounded.
- Bonded to Bram by accident when his blood drops into Bram's wound. Tried, and failed, to kill him.
- Woke first after the bonding and carried Bram to a new, more distant cave before more wizards could come.
- Refuses to let Bram ride him. Bram can find his own way or grow his own wings.
- Later injured or captured during Bram's attack on a human castle.

**Tension to resolve:** the original plot has them conquering dragons and expanding a domain, but Vharis wants nothing more than what he has. Who drives the expansion, and why does Vharis go along?

**Open questions**
- Sex, color, size?
- Why didn't he kill Bram after waking? Fear the bond would kill him too? Curiosity? Something he felt through the bond?
- **[suggestion]** The bond gives Vharis a second adolescence: a late, unwanted surge of the ambition young dragons get. He resents it, and it's the engine for expansion.

## Queen Ysolde (POV)
- Incredibly smart, very detail oriented.
- Mostly frustrated with people who don't believe in her and doubt her capabilities no matter what she does. Not framed as a sexism story; the doubt has other roots.

**Open questions**
- Which kingdom does she rule? Is it the kingdom Bram later attacks? Orvane's kingdom?
- Why do people doubt her? (Young? Came to the throne irregularly? Believes in the apocalypse when others don't?)
- **[suggestion]** She is one of the few rulers who believes the apocalypse is real, which is exactly why no one takes her seriously.

## The bonding king (working name King Aldric)
- Obsessed with getting his soldiers bonded to dragons. Exhausts his kingdom's resources and men capturing dragons and wounding men to force the bond. It keeps failing.
- At the end of his storyline, his men turn on him, cut off his arms, and throw him to a captured dragon to be eaten. Instead, he and the dragon bond.

**Open questions**
- Does he survive? (See author's note: dragon eats his men and they escape?)
- Both arms, or one?
- Why did his bond finally work when hundreds of forced attempts failed? (See magic.md.)

## The school's dragon-hunting professor
- One of the most powerful wizards at the school. Finds the cave and marches the whole school out to kill the dragon, as a show of power.
- Working name: Archmagister Selen.

## Bandits
- Hired by Orvane. Arrive at the wrong time and/or are far more violent than he wanted. Kill the guards and horses.
