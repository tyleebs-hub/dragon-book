# World

## Scope
- One world. No other planes, no cosmic stakes. Everything happens here.
- Grounded, gritty, medieval-feeling.

## Politics
- Many kingdoms, ruled by ambitious men and women.
- Wizards are fiercely loyal and patriotic to their own kingdom. There is no brotherhood among magic users, only loyalty by country.
- One wizard wants to unite magic users across kingdoms to stand against the coming apocalypse. (Who? See open questions.)
- Most people, and most rulers, doubt the apocalypse is real.

## Places
- **Kell's Ford:** Bram and Hob's village, on a river with a ferry crossing. Drill ground behind Maddoc's house at the top of the street. Tannery at the downwind end. Marran's alehouse, a church with a poor spire, Jorrey's smithy. (Working name.)
- **Ferrin:** market town nine miles down the road from Kell's Ford. The Ferrin road leads on toward Dunmere; the levy comes up it.
- **Caldreth:** Orvane's kingdom, a large one. Badge: a black heron on a white ground. Raises a levy from the villages every third or fourth year. The current king believes in the apocalypse; his father did not. (Working name.)
- **The Narrows:** a battle of Maddoc's war, roughly twenty-plus years ago. The Third Foot under Harrow was there. Orvane came after and "saw what was left." Nothing more decided.
- **Dunmere:** capital of Caldreth, where the school is. Reached by carriage road from Kell's Ford. (Working name.)
- **The queen's kingdom:** neighbors Caldreth. Name TBD.
- **The school:** trains young wizards. A dragon cave lies unusually close to it.
- **Vharis's old cave:** near the school. Abandoned after the hunt.
- **Vharis's new cave:** farther away. Where Bram wakes after the bond.
- **Bram's domain and fortress:** later in the story.
- **The bonding king's kingdom:** where dragons are captured and men are wounded to force bonds. His castle is the one Bram attacks, to free the caged dragons he can feel there.
