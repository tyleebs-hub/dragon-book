# World

## Scope
- One world. No other planes, no cosmic stakes. Everything happens here.
- Grounded, gritty, medieval-feeling.

## Politics
- Many kingdoms, ruled by ambitious men and women.
- Wizards are fiercely loyal and patriotic to their own kingdom. There is no brotherhood among magic users, only loyalty by country.
- One wizard wants to unite magic users across kingdoms to stand against the coming apocalypse. (Who? See open questions.)
- Most people, and most rulers, doubt the apocalypse is real.
