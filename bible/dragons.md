# Dragons

## Nature
- Most dragons are lazy, hoarding, and territorial. They guard what they have and do little else.
- Just after adolescence, a dragon has a short window of fierce productivity, power, and ambition. Dragons in this phase are the dangerous ones.
- Because most dragons humans encounter are lazy, most people doubt the apocalypse.
- Dragons speak telepathically. (Render in *italics*, no quotation marks.)

## The apocalypse
- A coming event in which evil dragons will attack humanity.
- Some wizards and rulers believe in it and prepare. Most doubt it.
