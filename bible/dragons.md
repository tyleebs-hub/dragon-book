# Dragons

## Nature
- Most dragons are lazy, hoarding, and territorial. They guard what they have and do little else.
- Just after adolescence, a dragon has a short window of fierce productivity, power, and ambition. Dragons in this phase are the dangerous ones.
- Because most dragons humans encounter are lazy, most people doubt the apocalypse.
- Dragons speak telepathically. (Render in *italics*, no quotation marks.)

## The apocalypse
- A coming event in which evil dragons will attack humanity.
- Some wizards and rulers believe in it and prepare. Most doubt it.

**Orvane's account (Ch 2, his claim, may be wrong or partial):** a great many dragons hatched within a few years of one another long ago; the season of ambition comes near enough to a fixed age that a man who can count can guess when; the king of Caldreth believes this, his father did not. Caldreth has lost a valley to a dragon in its season twice in living memory.

**Open questions**
- What triggers it? (Orvane's version above is one answer; the author has not confirmed it as the truth of the world.) Many young dragons coming of age at once? A leader dragon? A cycle?
- What makes a dragon "evil"? Is it simply the ambitious post-adolescent phase, or something more?
- (Decided) This is a series. Book one ends at the apocalypse's first strike, right after Orvane's confrontation and the castle attack, with Bram proven right at the moment he is weakest.
