# Outline

Working structure. Beats come from the author's notes; anything marked **[suggestion]** is Claude's and needs approval. Chapter numbers are rough and will shift.

## Part One: The Village and the Road

**Ch 1 (Hob POV).** Kell's Ford. Maddoc's drill ground. We see Bram only from outside: strong, quiet, maybe frightening. The phrase, "Never stay down," and what it means to Hob. A sign of Bram's spark in a bout. Hob's crowded home and his wanting out. Word arrives that a wizard from Dunmere is two days behind on the road, looking at villages that train.

**Ch 2 (Hob POV).** Orvane arrives. The village performs for him. Hob wants to be chosen; Orvane's eyes stay on Bram. Orvane asks to see the boys fight.

**Ch 3 (Hob POV).** Hob draws Bram. The bout goes long. Bram could stop and does not; at the end his spark flares. Hob dies from his own POV.

**Ch 4 (Bram POV).** Same events, other side. Bram's reading of the phrase. His doubt: how much of it was him. Orvane's offer, which is also the village's way to be rid of him. Leaving Tobin.

**Ch 5 to 6 (Bram POV).** The carriage journey to Dunmere. Orvane smug and lecturing. The staged ambush goes wrong: Orvane in his slumber, bandits too early and too violent (someone paid them more), guards and horses killed, Bram kills several bandits.

**Ch 7 (Orvane or Bram POV).** Orvane wakes to the mess. **[suggestion]** Open with Orvane mid-slumber, boasting to fellow wizards about his boy, then cut to him waking. They continue on foot.

## Part Two: The School

**Ch 8 to 10 (Bram POV).** Arrival at the capital and school. Bram struggles in his studies. Rivals, maybe a friend.

**Queen chapters (interleaved).** Introduce the queen and the politics: kingdoms, patriotic wizards, doubt about the apocalypse. She rules a kingdom neighboring Caldreth and is one of the few rulers who believes, which is why no one takes her seriously.

**Ch 11 to 13 (Bram POV).** Archmagister Selen finds the dragon cave. The school marches out. Bram on the front line in anti-dragon armor, as bait. Vharis flies out above them and devastates the expedition. Bram starts to sympathize with the beast.

**Ch 14 (Bram POV).** Bram wakes among the dead and retreating. Vharis wounded, licking his wounds. Bram walks under him to finish him. Blood falls into Bram's wound. The bond. Vharis tries and fails to kill him. Both collapse.

## Part Three: The Bond

**Ch 15 to 18 (Bram POV, maybe Vharis's voice in dialogue).** Bram wakes in the back of a new cave. Vharis carried him there. Exploring the bond: what it is, what it costs, what each wants. Vharis refuses to be ridden. Vharis wants his quiet life back.

**Bonding king chapters (interleaved, officer POV).** Told by an officer in the king's service who will later be among the men who maim him. A king wounding men and caging dragons, failing again and again. Mirrors Bram's accidental bond.

## Part Four: The Domain

**Beats (order TBD).**
- Bram and Vharis conquer other dragons and claim territory. Bram drives it. Vharis helps grudgingly, to keep the boy alive, and never admits he cares.
- Bram builds and fortifies a fortress.
- Humans begin to join him.
- Orvane comes to the fortress and confronts him. Bram: I've done more to prepare than anyone.
- Bram learns to fly within the domain.

## Part Five: The Castle

**Beats (order TBD).**
- Bram attacks the bonding king's castle because he can feel the caged dragons there.
- Vharis is captured in the attack. Bram loses most of his power until he gets back into his domain.
- The bonding king's men turn on him, maim him, and throw him to a dragon. The king and dragon bond. **Open questions:** does he survive and escape; both arms or one.

## Ending
**Decided.** This is a series. Book one ends at the apocalypse's first strike, right after Orvane's confrontation and the castle attack: Bram is proven right at the moment he is weakest.
