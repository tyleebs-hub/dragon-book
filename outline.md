# Outline

Working structure. Beats come from the author's notes; anything marked **[suggestion]** is Claude's and needs approval. Chapter numbers are rough and will shift.

## Part One: The Village and the Road

**Ch 1 to 2 (Tam POV).** Village training group. We see Bram only from outside: strong, quiet, maybe frightening. The group's phrase. Signs of Bram's spark. Orvane arrives looking for talent.

**Ch 3 (Tam POV).** Tam dies at Bram's hand. **[suggestion]** A fight or sparring match that goes too far, possibly tangled up with Bram's spark.

**Ch 4 (Bram POV).** Same events, other side. Bram's reading of the phrase. His doubt and guilt. Why he has to go with Orvane.

**Ch 5 to 6 (Bram POV).** The carriage journey to the capital. Orvane smug and lecturing. The staged ambush goes wrong: Orvane in his slumber, bandits too early and too violent, guards and horses killed, Bram kills several bandits.

**Ch 7 (Orvane or Bram POV).** Orvane wakes to the mess. **[suggestion]** Open with Orvane mid-slumber, boasting to fellow wizards about his boy, then cut to him waking. They continue on foot.

## Part Two: The School

**Ch 8 to 10 (Bram POV).** Arrival at the capital and school. Bram struggles in his studies. Rivals, maybe a friend.

**Queen chapters (interleaved).** Introduce the queen and the politics: kingdoms, patriotic wizards, doubt about the apocalypse. **Open question:** where she sits relative to Orvane's kingdom.

**Ch 11 to 13 (Bram POV).** Archmagister Selen finds the dragon cave. The school marches out. Bram on the front line in anti-dragon armor, as bait. Vharis flies out above them and devastates the expedition. Bram starts to sympathize with the beast.

**Ch 14 (Bram POV).** Bram wakes among the dead and retreating. Vharis wounded, licking his wounds. Bram walks under him to finish him. Blood falls into Bram's wound. The bond. Vharis tries and fails to kill him. Both collapse.

## Part Three: The Bond

**Ch 15 to 18 (Bram POV, maybe Vharis's voice in dialogue).** Bram wakes in the back of a new cave. Vharis carried him there. Exploring the bond: what it is, what it costs, what each wants. Vharis refuses to be ridden. Vharis wants his quiet life back.

**Bonding king chapters (interleaved, POV TBD).** A king wounding men and caging dragons, failing again and again. Mirrors Bram's accidental bond.

## Part Four: The Domain

**Beats (order TBD).**
- Bram and Vharis conquer other dragons and claim territory. **Open question:** what drives an old, content dragon to expand.
- Bram builds and fortifies a fortress.
- Humans begin to join him.
- Orvane comes to the fortress and confronts him. Bram: I've done more to prepare than anyone.
- Bram learns to fly within the domain.

## Part Five: The Castle

**Beats (order TBD).**
- Bram attacks a human castle. **Open question:** why, and whose.
- Vharis is injured or captured. Bram loses most of his power until he gets back into his domain.
- The bonding king's men turn on him, maim him, and throw him to a dragon. The king and dragon bond. **Open questions:** does he survive and escape; both arms or one.
