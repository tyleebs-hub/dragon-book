# CLAUDE.md: Standing Rules for This Book

This repo is a novel in progress. Read this file at the start of every session.

## Before writing anything
1. Read `continuity.md` in full.
2. Read the outline beat for the chapter you are writing in `outline.md`.
3. Read the previous chapter in `chapters/` (at minimum its last 2 scenes), and the last chapter from the same POV character if different.
4. Read every `bible/` file relevant to the chapter. When in doubt, read `bible/magic.md`; the bond and domain rules are where drift happens.

## While writing
- One chapter per session. Target 3,000 to 5,000 words unless the outline says otherwise.
- Never contradict the bible. If the story needs a rule to change, stop and ask the author first; do not quietly change it in prose.
- Do not resolve anything listed under "Open questions" in a bible file or the outline without asking. Write around it or ask.
- Do not introduce a major new character, power, or faction without asking.

## After writing
1. Save the chapter as `chapters/NN-short-title.md` (two-digit number). Put the POV character's name under the chapter title.
2. Append to `continuity.md`: a 3 to 6 sentence chapter summary, plus any new facts (injuries, deaths, who knows what, items, promises, relationship shifts, setups that need payoff).
3. If a bible fact was established in prose (a place name, a minor character), add it to the right bible file.
4. List anything you were unsure about at the end of your reply so the author can rule on it.

## Voice and style
- POV: third person limited. One POV character per chapter (or per clearly marked section). Rotating POVs: see `bible/characters.md` for who gets one.
- Each POV character sees the world differently. Bram's chapters should read differently from the queen's or Hob's. Characters can be wrong about each other; that is the point.
- Tense: past.
- Tone: grounded, gritty, real. Detailed but never boring: specific physical detail, no inventory lists or travelogue.
- Magic is limited, costly, and strange. It is often not the best option. Show people solving problems without it.
- No cosmic scope. No other planes, gods descending, or universe-level stakes. Everything happens on this world.
- Death is real. Characters can die, including POV characters, including from their own perspective. Do not soften or reverse deaths.
- Sexual content: present but limited. Brief, not explicit, and only where it matters to character.
- Dialogue does the heavy lifting; keep exposition short and earned.
- Dragons speak telepathically. Render dragon speech in *italics*, no quotation marks.
- **Never use em dashes.** Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses instead.
- Avoid: "a testament to," "little did he know," characters "letting out a breath they didn't know they were holding," and stacked adjectives.

## Continuity checks
When asked for a continuity pass, read the target chapter against `bible/` and `continuity.md` and report contradictions as a list. Do not rewrite unless asked.
